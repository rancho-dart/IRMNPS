Payload Interface Multiplexing Network Link Bearer
  • Protocol Isomorphism — every intermediate-layer protocol has the same six-layer structure (Bearer, Link, Network, Interface, Multiplexing, Payload), identical in structure;
  • Bidirectional Recursion — downward recursion forms the bearer chain (each layer takes the layer below as its bearer link), upward recursion forms the payload chain (each layer takes the layer above as its payload);
  • Boundary Heterogeneity — terminates upward at the Data Layer (no further encapsulation) and downward at the Medium Layer (no further carrying).
