🛡️ HTTP over VPN

Every intermediate protocol carries its own four-part structure — a data link layer (communication between directly adjacent nodes) and a network layer (forwarding between non-adjacent nodes).
All protocols above a given layer are uniformly treated as its transport layer; all protocols below it are uniformly treated as its bearer layer.
The topmost data layer has no internal sublayers, and the bottommost layer has no bearer — the two termination conditions of the recursion.
Here HTTP runs on top of a VPN tunnel: the inner stack (HTTP → TCP → Inner IP) is carried by the tunnel, and the tunnel itself is carried over the public internet and the physical medium (UDP → Outer IP → Ethernet → Coding/Modulation → Medium).
