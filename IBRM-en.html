Transport Network Data Link Bearer
  • Isomorphism — every middle-layer protocol has the same internal four-layer structure (Bearer, Data Link, Network, Transport), structurally identical;
  • Bidirectional Recursion — downward recursion forms the bearer chain (each layer treats the layer below as its bearer link); upward recursion forms the transport chain (each layer treats the layer above as payload);
  • Boundary Conditions — terminates upward at the Data Layer (no further encapsulation) and downward at the Medium Layer (no further bearer).
